Our Philosophy

Not another Islamic app.
A new foundation.

📱 Traditional Launcher
  • ✕ Optimized for screen time
  • ✕ No concept of prayer times
  • ✕ Every app equally accessible
  • ✕ Designed to maximize distraction
  • ✕ Tracks your behavior for ads
  • ✕ Gregorian-only calendar
VS
☽ Muslim Launcher
  • ✓ Home screen around Salah times
  • ✓ Prayer countdown always visible
  • ✓ Apps categorized by benefit
  • ✓ Intentional friction for wasteful apps
  • ✓ Zero analytics, zero telemetry
  • ✓ Hijri calendar as first citizen
Core Features

Built for Muslim life

🕌

Prayer-Centered Interface

Prayer time countdown prominently displayed. Auto-focus mode 5 minutes before each Salah. Hijri calendar alongside Gregorian. Jumah and Ramadan highlighted automatically.

Core
🚧

Digital Boundaries

Apps in three categories: Beneficial (green), Neutral (gray), Time-Wasting (red). Wasteful apps require an extra tap with usage stats before opening.

Boundaries
🧘

Focus Modes

Prayer Mode, Ramadan Mode, Night Guard, Work Mode. Powered by Muslim Services integration — only essential apps accessible during prayer windows.

Focus
🔐

Privacy First

No tracking. No analytics. All data stays on your device, encrypted. Works completely offline. Open source — code is publicly auditable.

Privacy
✨

Minimalist Design

No infinite scroll. No notification feeds cluttering the home screen. Clean, calm interface with a single intentional swipe away for everything else. Dark mode optimized.

Design
📋

Halal App Directory

Community-curated directory of privacy-respecting, ad-free, beneficial apps for Muslims. Developers can submit apps for review.
